### New
- **An update you won't miss.** Once a new version has downloaded, a "New update available" button stays in the toolbar, and "Install Update…" in the Xylem menu, until it's installed. One click shows what's new and installs it. Before, a single notification was easy to miss while Xylem stayed open for days.
- **What's new after an update.** The first launch after an update installed on quit shows its release notes once.

### Improved
- The panel button in the toolbar is now as tall as the other buttons.
- The About window is simpler: no site link, and even spacing.
