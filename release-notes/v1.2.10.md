### New
- **Spend limits behind a Claude gateway.** When your organization's Claude gateway sets a spend limit, the toolbar shows what you have spent and the limit, for example $12.40/$100, next to the plan limits. Hovering shows the period and when it resets.

### Improved
- **The model menu knows your plan.** A model your plan doesn't include, such as Opus on a Pro plan, is greyed out under "Not included in your plan" and left out of ⌘K, instead of being sent to Claude Code only to be refused.
- The hourglass next to the plan limits is the same grey as the numbers.

### Fixed
- The model and effort menus now show a checkmark at the current choice.
- After signing in to another Claude account, the toolbar no longer shows the plan limits of the previous one.
