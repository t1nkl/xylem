### Fixed
- **Project names.** Projects that have no saved sessions showed wrong names and paths: a folder like `billing-api` appeared as `api`, under a path that doesn't exist. Xylem now finds the real folder on disk, whatever its name (dashes, dots, spaces or non-Latin letters).
- **Smoother scrolling** through a long Recent Projects list: every card has the same height now ("No sessions" for a project without any).
- **Dates.** After a week, recent projects and sessions show the day ("Oct 20", or "Oct 20, 2025" for earlier years) instead of "20/10/26".
