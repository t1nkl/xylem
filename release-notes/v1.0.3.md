### New
- **Send crash reports switch.** Settings > General > Privacy lets you turn crash reports off (they are on by default and take effect after a restart). Reports never include your prompts or project names.

### Improved
- **Recent Projects fill the window.** The New Tab screen shows as many full rows of projects as fit on your screen, with a "Show more" tile at the end when there are more.
- **No more piles of empty tabs.** New Tab takes you to a tab that is still waiting for a folder instead of opening another one.
- **Full first prompt on hover.** In the Sessions list, hovering a session shows its whole first message, not just the first line.
- **Hover feedback** on tabs, the tab close button, Open Folder, Command Palette, All Sessions and Doctor.
- **Cleaner prompt search.** Cmd+K no longer lists prompts from temporary folders left behind by other tools.
- **Tidier welcome screens.** The Resume button names the project instead of quoting your last prompt, and the New Tab screen sits higher with less empty space.
- The Settings window opens centred on the screen and fits the General tab without scrolling.
