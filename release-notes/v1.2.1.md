### New
- **A loader while a tab starts.** A new tab, or Xylem opening with your tabs, shows a small spinner and "Starting Claude Code…" instead of a blank terminal, until Claude draws its first screen. If your shell asks you something while it starts, the terminal shows it right away.

### Improved
- **One notification per thing Claude does.** Resizing the window or opening a side panel no longer sends the same "Claude finished" again. A turn Claude starts on its own (a background task reporting back) finishes quietly unless you wrote something since; `/goal` still tells you when it's done.
- The About window is shorter (licenses stay in Help > Open-Source Licenses), and its copyright year follows the calendar.

### Fixed
- A tab stopped with Esc could be reported as finished about a minute later.
